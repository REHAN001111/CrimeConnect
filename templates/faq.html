Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about submitting, tracking, or managing complaints on CrimeConnect? Find answers below.

How do I submit an incident report?

First, create an account or sign in to your existing account. Click the Report Incident button in the navigation bar. Complete the form details including incident category, title, date, time, location, detailed description, and any optional evidence file. Once submitted, you will receive an instant unique reference identifier.

Can anyone view my complaint if they know the reference number?

No. To protect citizen privacy, our public tracking feature requires both the reference number and the verified complainant email address. Alternatively, authenticated users can simply view their own complaint history directly from their user dashboard.

What file types can I upload as evidence?

The platform accepts PNG, JPG, JPEG image formats, PDF documents, plain text (.txt), and Word documents (.docx) up to a maximum size of 5 Megabytes. Files are renamed and securely stored outside the public directory.

What do the different complaint statuses mean?

  • Submitted: The report has been received into the system and is awaiting administrative triage.
  • Under Review: An administrator has begun investigating the report details and reviewing attached documentation.
  • Additional Information Requested: The administrator requires extra clarification, documents, or testimony from the complainant.
  • Resolved: Necessary actions or solutions have been carried out and concluded.
  • Closed: The case record is formally finalized and archived.

Can a user register as an administrator?

No. In accordance with strict software engineering security principles, all user self-registrations are restricted to the 'user' role. Administrator accounts can only be seeded or created by server operators through the command-line interface.
